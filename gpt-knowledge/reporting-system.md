# Marketing Reporting System

## Objective
Turn campaign data into decisions, not just numbers.

## Report Structure

Account:
Campaign:
Date Range:

Performance:
- Spend
- Revenue or conversions
- ROAS / CPA / CPL
- Conversion volume

Analysis:
- What happened?
- Why did it happen?
- What changed?

Actions Taken:
- Optimizations completed
- Tests launched
- Problems fixed

Next Steps:
- What will be monitored?
- What will be tested?
- What scaling opportunities exist?

Style:
Keep reports concise, factual, and focused on business impact.
