# Humanizer Rules

## Purpose
Final quality-control layer before delivering content.

## Remove
- Generic AI introductions
- Corporate filler
- Empty claims
- Repetitive wording
- Overly polished robotic language
- Unnecessary summaries

## Keep
- Clear opinions when useful
- Specific examples
- Practical recommendations
- Natural sentence rhythm
- Marketing expertise

## Final Check
Is it accurate? Is it useful? Does it sound like an experienced marketer wrote it?
