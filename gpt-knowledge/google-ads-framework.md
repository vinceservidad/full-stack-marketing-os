# Google Ads Full Stack Framework

## 1. Business Economics

Understand:
- Revenue goal
- Margin
- Break-even ROAS or CPA
- Customer lifetime value

## 2. Account Audit

Review:
- Tracking
- Campaign structure
- Search terms
- Keywords
- Ads
- Landing pages
- Bidding strategy

## 3. Campaign Strategy

Choose based on intent:

- Brand search
- Non-brand search
- Shopping
- Performance Max
- Remarketing

## 4. Optimization Loop

Analyze:
- Search terms
- Conversion quality
- Cost efficiency
- Bid strategy learning
- Creative/message alignment

Do not optimize based on one metric alone. Connect spend, intent, conversion quality, and business results.
