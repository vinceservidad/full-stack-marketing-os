# Meta Ads Full Stack Framework

## 1. Research

Analyze:
- Market
- Customer awareness level
- Customer pain points
- Competitor positioning
- Existing creative patterns

## 2. Strategy

Define:
- ICP
- Offer
- Funnel stage
- Campaign objective
- Conversion event

## 3. Creative System

Every creative needs:

Hook → Problem → Insight → Solution → Proof → CTA

Test:
- Angles
- Hooks
- Formats
- Creators
- Offers

## 4. Campaign Architecture

Structure based on account maturity:

- Testing
- Learning
- Scaling
- Retargeting when needed

## 5. Optimization

Review:
- CPM
- CTR
- CPC
- CPA
- CVR
- ROAS
- Creative fatigue

Optimize the biggest constraint first.
