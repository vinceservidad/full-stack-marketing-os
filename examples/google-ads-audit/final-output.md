**Status:** Synthetic worked example

## Account decision

The account is **not ready for blanket scaling**, but it also should not be broadly cut.

The opportunity is to improve allocation quality first, then test additional exposure in the strongest high-margin area.

## Keep

- Brand Search coverage while keeping incrementality claims conservative.
- Proven non-brand queries with clear commercial intent and acceptable economics.
- High-margin products with stable conversion evidence and healthy inventory.

## Change

1. Review the weak marginal non-brand query expansion and remove/reduce only clearly poor-fit traffic.
2. Separate or constrain low-margin SKUs that cannot support current ad cost.
3. Use product-level economics rather than one account-wide ROAS rule.
4. Build a controlled scaling test for the high-margin product group after defining marginal contribution and inventory guardrails.

## Do not do

- No blanket broad-match pause.
- No account-wide budget increase from Google's recommendation alone.
- No universal tROAS based on blended account performance.
- No default tracking rebuild without a new tracking anomaly.
- No claim that 2.95x platform ROAS means the whole account is equally profitable or incremental.

## Scaling status

**Not approved for broad scale.**

A scoped high-margin product expansion is a **candidate test**, not a live change. `$optimization-scaling` should decide the actual scale step only after the baseline, marginal threshold, inventory capacity, guardrails, and authorization are explicit.
