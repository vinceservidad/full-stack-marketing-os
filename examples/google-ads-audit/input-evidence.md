## Commercial inputs

- High-margin product group: 58% gross margin before ad spend.
- Low-margin product group: 32% gross margin before ad spend.
- Shipping and variable fulfillment differ by SKU.
- Finance-defined contribution target is supplied by product group; there is no single universal account ROAS target.

## Search/query evidence

- Non-brand broad queries expanded materially after budget increases.
- A subset of terms has strong conversion volume and commercial fit.
- A second subset is informational/adjacent and consumes 21% of non-brand spend with weak direct conversion evidence.
- No evidence is supplied that all broad match is bad or that all exact match is profitable.

## Product evidence

- Four low-margin SKUs receive disproportionate Shopping/PMax spend relative to contribution.
- Two high-margin products have stable conversion volume but are budget-constrained during several high-demand periods.
- Inventory is healthy for the high-margin products.

## Measurement state

- Primary ecommerce purchase tracking is supplied as established and stable in this synthetic scenario.
- Platform revenue is treated as Google Ads-reported attribution, not automatically as incremental business revenue.
- No new anomaly indicates a conversion-tracking failure.

## Unknowns

- True incrementality of brand Search and remarketing.
- Whether some weak non-brand terms assist later conversions beyond currently available evidence.
- The exact marginal response if budget is shifted toward high-margin products.

## Platform recommendation note

The account includes automated recommendations to increase budgets and broaden reach. These are platform suggestions, not evidence that the business should accept them.
