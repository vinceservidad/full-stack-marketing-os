# Ecommerce Growth Diagnosis — Worked Example

**Status:** Synthetic worked example

This example shows how Full-Stack Marketing OS diagnoses a slowing ecommerce business without jumping straight to channel tactics.

## Starting request

> Revenue has flattened even though ad spend increased. Diagnose the growth problem, tell me what to prioritize, and tell me what not to do yet.

## Business

Fictional brand: **Northstar Home**

Product category: home organization products

Primary market: United States

Business model: Shopify ecommerce, mostly first-order acquisition with some repeat purchase.

All numbers and business details in this example are synthetic and exist only to demonstrate the operating system.

## Owner chain

```text
$marketing-intake
→ $growth-strategy
→ $performance-diagnostics
→ $google-ads + $meta-ads + $creative-strategy + $cro
→ $tracking-measurement
→ $optimization-scaling only if readiness is established
```

`$growth-strategy` owns the business-level priority and opportunity portfolio. Channel skills still own channel decisions.

## Learning objective

The example demonstrates that a revenue plateau can be caused by several interacting constraints. It does not assume that more spend, a site redesign, a new channel, or a creative refresh is automatically the answer.
