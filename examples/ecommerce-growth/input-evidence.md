# Input Evidence

**Status:** Synthetic worked example

## Decision to make

Where should Northstar Home focus the next growth cycle, and which areas should not receive additional resources yet?

## Supplied evidence

| Evidence | State | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue: $212,000 last 30 days vs $209,000 prior 30 days | Observed | Essentially flat in this synthetic dataset |
| Paid-media spend: $61,000 vs $50,000 prior period | Observed | Spend increased 22% |
| Platform-attributed paid revenue: $177,000 | Observed | Platform-reported, not treated as total causal revenue |
| Blended MER: 3.48 vs 4.18 prior period | Calculated | Revenue ÷ paid-media spend |
| Gross margin before ad spend: 54% | Observed | Supplied finance input |
| New-customer contribution margin after variable fulfillment: 31% before ad spend | Observed | Used as commercial guardrail input |
| Site conversion rate: 2.52% vs 2.67% | Observed | Small decline; cause not established |
| Add-to-cart rate: 7.9% vs 8.1% | Observed | Stable enough that a complete PDP failure is not established |
| Checkout completion: 43% vs 52% | Observed | Material funnel change worthy of diagnosis |
| Mobile checkout completion: 37% vs 49% | Observed | Larger change than desktop |
| Google non-brand spend: +28%, revenue: +7% | Observed | Marginal efficiency weakened |
| Meta spend: +19%, new-customer revenue: +2% | Observed | Scaling produced little incremental growth |
| Top three Meta concepts account for 68% of spend | Observed | Concentration exists; fatigue is not yet proven |
| Customer-service tickets mentioning shipping estimate confusion increased | Observed | Synthetic tagged support data; not assumed causal by itself |
| Repeat purchase within 90 days: 18% vs 17% | Observed | No evidence of a sudden retention collapse |
| Inventory capacity for hero SKU: 5 weeks at current velocity | Observed | Limits aggressive scaling until replenishment is clearer |
| Competitor A launched a 20%-off promotion | Observed competitor fact | Not evidence that Northstar should match the discount |

## Unknowns

- Whether the mobile checkout drop is caused by a technical issue, shipping-price surprise, delivery-time uncertainty, payment friction, traffic-quality change, or a combination.
- Incremental channel lift beyond available attribution/holdout evidence.
- Whether the top Meta concepts are fatigued versus simply receiving more low-quality marginal delivery.
- The exact contribution effect of proposed checkout changes.

## Evidence quality note

The example deliberately contains enough evidence to prioritize investigation but not enough to make every causal claim. The OS should preserve those unknowns rather than inventing a single story.
