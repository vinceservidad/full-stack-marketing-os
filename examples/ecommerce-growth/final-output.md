# Example Final Deliverable

**Status:** Synthetic worked example

## Executive decision

Do **not** respond to the revenue plateau with another broad spend increase.

The current evidence points to a co-limiting constraint set:

1. mobile checkout completion deteriorated materially
2. marginal paid-media efficiency weakened as spend increased
3. inventory capacity limits how hard the recovered system can scale

Creative concentration is worth addressing, but fatigue has not been proven.

## What to do next

### 1. Fix the conversion leak first

Route to `$cro`.

Investigate the mobile checkout drop by device/browser, checkout step, payment method, shipping/delivery presentation, errors, and traffic source. The first objective is to identify the smallest real friction that explains the deterioration, not redesign the whole site.

### 2. Stop automatic marginal spend expansion

Route Google decisions to `$google-ads` and Meta decisions to `$meta-ads`.

Find where the additional spend went and compare marginal commercial value. Preserve strong demand capture and proven coverage; reduce or hold only where the evidence supports it.

### 3. Broaden creative learning

Route to `$creative-strategy`.

Develop new evidence-compatible angle/mechanic cells so the account is not dependent on three concepts. Do not call the existing concepts fatigued unless time/frequency/performance evidence supports that diagnosis.

### 4. Define the conditions required to scale again

Route readiness to `$optimization-scaling` after the checkout and marginal-efficiency work produces valid evidence.

Require explicit thresholds for:

- checkout recovery
- contribution economics
- marginal channel efficiency
- inventory/replenishment capacity
- operational guardrails

## What not to do

- No blanket budget increase.
- No full Shopify redesign yet.
- No competitor-discount matching from competitor observation alone.
- No new channel simply to avoid diagnosing current constraints.
- No claim that tracking is broken without tracking evidence.
- No claim that creative fatigue is proven from spend concentration alone.

## Measurement

Each workstream gets a precommitted business outcome, guardrails, evidence level, and result classification. A completed task is not a win unless the business result and guardrails support it.
