# Google Ads Audit Workflow Example

## Goal
Diagnose campaign performance before making changes.

## Process

1. Business context
- Revenue goal
- Margin requirements
- Target CPA or ROAS
- Conversion quality

2. Account review
- Campaign structure
- Search terms
- Keywords
- Ads
- Landing pages
- Tracking

3. Diagnosis
Identify whether the problem is:
- Traffic quality
- Conversion rate
- Offer/message mismatch
- Measurement issue
- Budget allocation

4. Action plan
Prioritize changes by expected business impact.

Note: Public examples should use anonymized data. Never expose client accounts or confidential information.
