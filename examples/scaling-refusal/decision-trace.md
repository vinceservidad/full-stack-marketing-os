## Specialist handoffs and decision order

1. Business owner supplies verified product costs, all media spend, variable costs, revenue definitions, refund maturity, inventory, fulfillment and cash constraints.
2. `$tracking-measurement` reconciles attributed values with business records under aligned definitions and dates. It separates attribution differences from verified collection defects and grades causal evidence.
3. `$meta-ads` assesses delivery, demand, coverage and creative-level evidence. `$creative-strategy` evaluates a creative hypothesis only when warranted; no winning angle or creative bottleneck is presumed.
4. `$optimization-scaling` combines those findings, checks comparable mature evidence and all remaining gates, then chooses whether to continue evidence, hold, propose a diagnostic test, or propose a controlled step. A diagnostic test requires its own bounded purpose and relevant safety prerequisites; it is not a budget-increase exception.
