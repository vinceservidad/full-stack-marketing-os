# Example Final Deliverable

**Status:** Synthetic worked example

## CRO decision

Do **not** start with a full Shopify redesign.

The strongest evidence is later in the funnel: add-to-cart is stable, while checkout completion fell materially and the decline is concentrated on mobile.

## Priority 1 — mobile checkout friction

Investigate:

- when shipping cost becomes visible
- whether a factual delivery range is available before checkout uncertainty
- return-policy visibility
- promo-code field salience
- mobile rendering/payment/errors by device/browser

## First hypothesis to validate

Surface concise, approved shipping/delivery and returns information before the buyer reaches the current uncertainty point.

If the checkout runtime cannot be changed directly, place the information in the closest truthful pre-checkout surface while preserving the same hypothesis.

## Preserve for now

- current hero/product explanation
- visible price
- simple variant selection
- primary add-to-cart treatment

Those elements may still have future opportunities, but the supplied data does not make them the first priority.

## Do not do yet

- no complete theme redesign
- no base-price change
- no invented “fast shipping” promise
- no hidden material fees
- no five-variable checkout redesign in one experiment

## Measurement

Primary outcome: mobile checkout-to-purchase completion.

Guardrails: contribution, refunds/cancellations, support burden, technical errors, desktop performance, and order-value effects where relevant.

## Current status

Draft CRO recommendation only. Policy truth, technical feasibility, approval, implementation, and live verification are still required.
