## Meta Ads audit summary

The account is **not ready for another blanket spend increase yet**.

The main issue is not one obvious “bad campaign.” The evidence points to four separate decisions that need to stay separate:

1. Meta-attributed revenue ($28.7k) does not yet reconcile with tagged store analytics ($22.4k), so platform ROAS should not be promoted to business truth.
2. Prospecting is weaker than the blended account number suggests because retargeting is carrying a higher platform ROAS.
3. C02 gets the highest CTR, but it currently sends lower-quality traffic downstream than C01.
4. Retargeting frequency is rising while response falls, which makes saturation/fatigue plausible but not yet proven.

## What I would keep

- Keep **C01** as the current control. It has the strongest purchase quality in this synthetic snapshot despite not having the highest CTR.
- Keep prospecting and retargeting evaluated separately.
- Keep the current audience conclusions provisional because broad and interest ad sets received different creative mixes.

## What I would investigate first

**1. Measurement reconciliation**

Confirm attribution windows, purchase-event integrity/deduplication, and the business source of truth for realized revenue and contribution.

**2. C02 message quality**

Its “what fits” hook gets attention, but the page immediately emphasizes appearance/material rather than capacity. Test message continuity before assuming the video format itself is bad.

**3. Retargeting delivery**

Inspect eligible audience size, exclusions, creative concentration, and conversion quality before calling the frequency increase “creative fatigue.”

## Next creative test

Use C01 as the control and test two genuinely different challengers:

- **Capacity Clarity:** demonstrate exactly what the organizer can hold and continue that promise on the destination.
- **Flexible Organization:** demonstrate removable/reconfigurable sections as a different reason to buy.

Do not make five cosmetic hook variants and call them five new concepts.

## Scaling decision

**Hold further scaling for now.**

Before `$optimization-scaling` considers another increase, require:

- reconciled business measurement
- new-customer contribution/economics
- stable enough evidence across the current spend level
- clear creative capacity
- a defined downside/rollback threshold

## What I would not do

- I would not pause C01 because C02 has a higher CTR.
- I would not call broad targeting the winner from this confounded snapshot.
- I would not rebuild the account into many small ad sets.
- I would not assume high retargeting ROAS means the account is healthy.
- I would not blame an undocumented Meta algorithm change.
- I would not change budget, audiences, ads, or statuses without approval.
