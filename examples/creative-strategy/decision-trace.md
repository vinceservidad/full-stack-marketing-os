## Three strategically distinct cells

### Cell A — Retrieval Relief

**Insight:** The frustration is not carrying items; it is repeatedly searching for them.

**Angle:** Stop digging through your own bag.

**Mechanic:** Controlled contrast.

**Concept:** Split visual meaning without copying a reference layout: one side shows an intentionally messy open tote; the other shows CarryKind organized with keys, bottle, laptop, and small essentials visibly assigned.

**Hook:** `YOUR BAG SHOULDN'T BE A SEARCH MISSION.`

**Proof:** Visual organization using truthful product compartments. No invented speed statistic.

**CTA:** `See CarryKind`

### Cell B — One Bag, Different Day

**Insight:** The same buyer may need structure during one part of the day and open space during another.

**Angle:** Organization when you want it. Open space when you don't.

**Mechanic:** Direct demonstration.

**Concept:** Show organizer modules installed, then one module lifted/removed with the same bag becoming more open. The visual demonstrates the real product feature rather than asserting abstract versatility.

**Hook:** `YOUR DAY CHANGES. YOUR BAG CAN TOO.`

**Proof:** Removable organizer modules shown accurately.

**CTA:** `See How It Works`

### Cell C — Organized Without Looking Overbuilt

**Insight:** Some buyers want practical organization but do not want a visibly utilitarian bag.

**Angle:** Structure inside. Everyday tote outside.

**Mechanic:** Implied conclusion.

**Concept:** Clean lifestyle composition where the exterior reads as a simple everyday tote while a controlled open-bag view reveals organization inside.

**Hook:** `THE ORGANIZED BAG THAT DOESN'T LOOK LIKE ONE.`

**Proof:** Accurate exterior/interior product view. No “premium,” fashion-status, or comparative superiority claim unless the brand supplies evidence/codes.

**CTA:** `Explore CarryKind`

## Controlled test design

Keep constant where practical:

- offer
- landing destination
- audience/placement treatment
- production quality
- product version
- measurement window

Primary learning variable: **angle/mechanic/concept cell**.

Do not treat hook synonyms or background colors as separate strategic cells.

## 4:5 production rule

For every 1080×1350 master that must survive a square derivative:

- centered square core = 1080×1080
- core vertical bounds = y=135 to y=1215
- top 135 px and bottom 135 px = crop-risk bands

Keep inside the square core:

- complete hook meaning
- essential product identity
- critical demonstrated feature
- material proof/offer qualifier
- CTA
- critical face/gesture if a person carries the concept

Allow outside the core only if expendable:

- background extension
- shadows
- atmosphere/texture
- nonessential props
- decorative product bleed that does not harm recognition

The actual 1:1 crop must be previewed. Guides alone do not prove crop safety.

## Render direction for Cell A

**Master:** 1080×1350 4:5.

**Reading order:** hook → visual contrast/product → CTA.

**Composition:** centered CarryKind product occupies the middle square-safe region. Messy-item tension is visible but contained enough that the square derivative retains the contrast. Keep the headline entirely below y=135 and above the product collision zone. CTA sits inside the lower portion of the square core, not in the bottom 135 px crop-risk band.

**Image generation:** generate the visual base without relying on the image model to render exact headline or CTA typography. Add exact text as an overlay afterward.

**Exclusions:** fake ratings, fake reviews, waterproof implications, impossible bag capacity, extra compartments not present on the product, copied competitor trade dress.

## What not to do

- Do not turn five headline rewrites into five “angles.”
- Do not call the 1:1 square a universal Meta safe zone.
- Do not place the CTA in the 4:5 bottom band if square survival is required.
- Do not use the synthetic review language as real public testimonials.
- Do not say the organization angle is proven before commercial testing.
- Do not use engagement rate alone to select the winner.
