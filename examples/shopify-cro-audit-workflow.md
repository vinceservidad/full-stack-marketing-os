# Shopify CRO Audit Workflow Example

## Goal
Find conversion opportunities before redesigning a store.

## Process

1. Research
- Customer intent
- Product positioning
- Traffic sources
- Customer objections

2. Audit journey
- Homepage
- Collection pages
- Product pages
- Cart
- Checkout

3. Identify friction
- Missing information
- Weak value proposition
- Trust gaps
- Poor mobile experience

4. Prioritize tests
Use impact, confidence, and effort to decide what to test first.

Public examples should demonstrate the framework without exposing private store data.
