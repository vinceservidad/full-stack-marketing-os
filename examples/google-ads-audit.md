# Google Ads Audit Example

## Objective

Identify opportunities to improve campaign efficiency, conversion quality, and scalability.

## Analysis Flow

1. Business economics
2. Conversion tracking validation
3. Account structure review
4. Search intent analysis
5. Keyword and query review
6. Ad messaging evaluation
7. Landing page assessment
8. Bidding and optimization review

## Output

The audit should produce:

- Findings
- Business impact
- Recommended actions
- Testing priorities
- Measurement plan

This example uses a framework approach and does not represent client data or guaranteed results.
