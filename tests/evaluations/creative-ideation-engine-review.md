# Creative Ideation Engine Evaluation Review

Reviewed: 2026-09-01

Records the required review of the Creative Ideation Engine evaluation specification. This review validates decision behavior, evidence handling, and authorization boundaries; it does not claim that any generated angle, hook, concept, or format will improve performance.

| Cases | Decision behavior | Evidence handling | Authorization boundary | Review result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–5 | Ideation begins from supplied or traceable audience/product evidence; awareness and belief states are not invented to force a message direction. | Unknown beliefs remain unknown or are labeled as hypotheses; product truth and proof availability constrain the angle. | Draft-only creative planning; no live campaign or asset change is authorized. | Pass |
| 6–10 | Expansion preserves the distinction between angle, hook, concept, format, proof, offer framing, and CTA; cosmetic rewrites are consolidated instead of counted as strategic breadth. | Generated ideas are treated as hypotheses, never as customer evidence or proof. | Selected cells still require approval and the existing creative-testing workflow before launch. | Pass |
| 11–15 | Test cells remain interpretable: meaningful variables are isolated, success signals and guardrails are defined, and unsupported claims or forced quotas are rejected. | Claims remain bounded by available substantiation; one win is not promoted to a durable pattern without replication. | No budget, bid, campaign, live creative, or publication state is changed by this workflow. | Pass |

Review conclusion: the 15 cases cover the main failure modes introduced by an ideation engine: fabricated psychology, quantity-over-quality generation, false strategic diversity, unsupported proof, uncontrolled testing, and premature claims of a winner. The implementation keeps ideation under `$creative-strategy`, routes selected cells into the existing controlled testing workflow, and preserves the repository's evidence and authorization rules.

Review result: **Pass**.
