# v1.18 Public Relations Evaluation Review

Reviewed: 2026-08-25

Records the required human review of the public relations evaluation specification. It does not claim any pitch, message, or crisis response guarantees coverage, sentiment, or a business outcome, and it does not substitute for legal review of a statement with real liability exposure.

| Cases | Decision behavior | Evidence handling | Authorization boundary | Review result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | Newsworthiness is assessed honestly before outreach; media-list fit is required; message control is never assumed over resulting coverage. | Claims to press are held to the same no-fabrication standard as every other deliverable in this system. | Not applicable. | Pass |
| 5–10 | Crisis responses state what is confirmed versus unknown rather than speculating; silence is not treated as a safe default; missed commitments and hindsight bias in review are both rejected. | Not applicable beyond factual-gap discipline. | Legal-exposure review runs in parallel with communications drafting, not after; an escalation/approval path is established before drafting proceeds. | Pass |
| 11–15 | Media mentions, correlated timing, and sentiment are never presented as proven business impact. | Correlated timing without a controlled design is capped at C1 on the causal ladder; composite scores are broken into their named components. | Incrementality claims route to `$tracking-measurement` rather than being asserted here. | Pass |
| 16–17 | Legal sufficiency is flagged for qualified review, never asserted as final. | Not applicable. | An escalating organic-social pattern hands off from routine community management to this skill's crisis discipline. | Pass |

Review conclusion: the 17 cases cover honest newsworthiness assessment, the crisis-communications discipline where speed, accuracy, and legal review all matter simultaneously under time pressure, and the weakest measurement-to-outcome connection of any channel in this system. Structural validation confirms `$public-relations` is packaged, registered, routed, cross-linked from `$organic-social`'s escalation path, and both references were linked from `SKILL.md` before the first validator run — no orphan this release, and the last of the originally identified unsupported channels is now governed. Whether a specific crisis response actually held to this discipline under real time pressure still requires review.
