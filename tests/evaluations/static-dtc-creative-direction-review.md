## Cross-crop decision review

Pass.

The method now distinguishes three layers:

1. **Master canvas** — the production aspect ratio and full composition.
2. **Cross-crop core** — a reusable internal region that preserves critical meaning when a known derivative crop such as 1:1 is required.
3. **Placement-specific safety** — current interface overlays, edge margins, or channel requirements verified separately.

For a 1080×1350 4:5 master with a required centered square derivative, the geometry is explicit: 1080×1080 core, 135 px crop-risk band above, and 135 px below. Hook/headline, essential product identity, material proof/qualifiers, CTA, and critical human features must survive the derivative. Expendable atmosphere and decorative bleed may use the crop-risk bands.

## Evidence and platform-currency review

Pass.

The 1:1 core is explicitly described as a cross-crop resilience rule, not a permanent platform specification. The method routes current placement-specific safe-area and UI-overlay claims to the relevant channel owner and `PLATFORM-CURRENCY.md`. This prevents a useful production heuristic from being fossilized into an unsupported platform fact.

## Final conclusion

**Pass.** The expanded 24-case suite preserves the original message, evidence, IP, test-design, and authorization guardrails while adding a clear, mathematically correct 4:5 → 1:1 crop-safety method and requiring actual derivative validation before approval.
