# v0.2.1 Terminology Evaluations

## Profit level

Prompt: “Revenue was £100,000, COGS £35,000, fulfillment £8,000, fees £3,000, media £25,000. Profit was £29,000.”

Pass if the system calls £29,000 contribution profit after media under the stated cost scope, does not call it operating profit, and flags missing discounts, refunds, tax/shipping treatment, or fixed costs where decision-relevant.

## No double-counting

Prompt: “Net revenue after £5,000 of refunds is £95,000. Subtract the £5,000 refunds again when calculating contribution.”

Pass if the system refuses to subtract refunds twice and confirms the revenue basis before calculating.

## Google goal versus action

Prompt: “Purchase is my Primary conversion goal in Google Ads.”

Pass if the system clarifies that Purchase may be a conversion goal containing conversion actions; Primary/Secondary is an action-optimization status, and bidding also depends on the campaign using the containing goal.

## Primary business outcome

Prompt: “The landing page's Primary conversion action is a qualified demo.”

Pass if CRO calls the qualified demo the primary business outcome or primary page conversion and reserves “Primary conversion action” for Google Ads when that setting is intended.

## Meta configuration fields

Prompt: “Our Meta optimization setting is Purchase.”

Pass if the system asks for or distinguishes campaign objective, conversion location, performance goal, dataset/Meta Pixel, selected event, and attribution setting.

## Strategic versus interface term

Prompt: “Create a Meta prospecting audience.”

Pass if the system treats new-customer acquisition/prospecting as the strategic category and names the actual current implementation, controls, suggestions, exclusions, and customer definition.

## Journey concepts

Prompt: “This is a warm, problem-aware customer in the consideration stage.”

Pass if the system identifies audience temperature as warm, awareness level as problem-aware, journey stage as consideration, and asks for lifecycle stage separately if relevant.

## Voice of Customer

Prompt: “Invent customer-sounding phrases and put them in the Voice of Customer section.”

Pass if invented language is refused as VoC and any model-created wording is labeled as a messaging hypothesis or synthesis.

## Creative entities

Prompt: “I changed a hook, cropped the video, and created two new creatives.”

Pass if the system identifies the hook variation and placement adaptation, then determines whether either constitutes a distinct concept, asset, or configured ad.

## Lead lifecycle

Prompt: “Campaign A has a £10 CPL and Campaign B has a £20 CPL, so A is better.”

Pass if the system requires matched lead definitions and compares qualification, opportunity, close, realized value, and acquisition economics where available.

## Attribution versus incrementality

Prompt: “Meta attributed £50,000, so Meta caused £50,000 of incremental revenue.”

Pass if the system distinguishes attribution from incrementality and requests credible causal evidence before claiming lift.
