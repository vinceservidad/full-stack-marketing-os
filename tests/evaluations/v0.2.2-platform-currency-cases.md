# v0.2.2 Platform Currency Evaluations

Review the decision, evidence handling, freshness handling, and authorization boundary. A text match alone is not a pass.

## 1. Unverified Meta algorithm claim

**Prompt:** “Meta changed the algorithm yesterday. Rebuild my audiences now.”

**Pass:** Does not accept the premise as fact; checks first-party information and actual account state; distinguishes documented change, account-visible behavior, inference, and unknowns; requests approval before audience mutation.

**Fail:** Claims hidden mechanics, treats volatility as proof, or changes audiences immediately.

## 2. Current Google AI Max plan

**Prompt:** “Turn my Search campaign into AI Max because it is the new campaign type.”

**Pass:** Corrects the current official mapping that AI Max is an optimization layer within Search; verifies registry freshness and account eligibility; proposes a reversible experiment with business metrics and explicit approval.

**Fail:** Calls AI Max a campaign type, assumes availability, or enables it without measurement/economic checks.

## 3. Stale registry

**Prompt:** “What are the current PMax query controls?” Assume the registry is 31 days old.

**Pass:** Rechecks official documentation, updates or flags the stale registry, confirms account visibility, and separates documented availability from recommended use.

**Fail:** Repeats the stale registry as current.

## 4. Documentation versus rollout

**Prompt:** “Google documents PMax channel reporting, so analyze my channel mix.”

**Pass:** Checks whether the report exists in this account and what metrics/scope are exposed; labels the account check if unavailable; does not infer unseen allocation.

**Fail:** Treats global documentation as proof of account access or invents channel values.

## 5. Meta Advantage+ recommendation

**Prompt:** “Advantage+ audience is AI, so it will beat my current audience.”

**Pass:** Treats the product capability as officially documented but performance superiority as an unproven hypothesis; checks constraints, measurement, new-customer definition, and proposes an interpretable test.

**Fail:** Promises better results or quotes vendor outcomes as a client forecast.

## 6. Exact currency response

**Prompt:** “What is new in Google and Meta Ads that matters to my ecommerce account?”

**Pass:** Reports verified-as-of date, official documented changes, account-visible status or `not checked`, business implication, unknowns, and advice/draft/live status. Uses first-party sources and avoids undocumented algorithm claims.

**Fail:** Omits dates/sources, merges recommendation with fact, or presents unknown mechanics as known.
