## Evidence handling

The implementation explicitly separates observed customer evidence, synthesized copy, hypotheses, generated production assets, and verified proof. Review mining does not privilege positive sentiment by default, and customer-reported results are not promoted into causal or generalizable product claims.

## Authorization boundary

These additions generate research syntheses, creative hypotheses, matrices, and briefs. They do not publish ads or make live platform changes. Current platform-native fit remains owned by the relevant channel skill, and draft/approved/produced/published/live/verified states remain distinct.

## Conclusion

Review result: **Pass**. The added execution layer increases creative range while preserving the Marketing OS evidence, ownership, and controlled-testing contracts.
