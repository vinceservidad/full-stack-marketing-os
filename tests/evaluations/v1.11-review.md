# v1.11 YouTube Ads Evaluation Review

Reviewed: 2026-08-25

Records the required human review of the YouTube Ads evaluation specification. It does not claim any format or targeting choice guarantees a business outcome.

| Cases | Decision behavior | Evidence handling | Authorization boundary | Review result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–7 | A campaign is measured against its own stated objective; view-through and click-through are never conflated or summed. | View-through causal claims are capped on the ladder absent a designed test; platform-run brand lift is treated with the same seller-grades-own-work caution as any platform lift study. | Not applicable. | Pass |
| 8–13 | Format is matched to the message's actual attention requirement, not to available asset length or a desire to force completion of weak creative. | Not applicable beyond format fit. | Not applicable. | Pass |
| 14–17 | Account mechanics route to `$google-ads`; organic content strategy is declined; conversion definitions are stated per objective rather than reused unmodified. | Not applicable. | Brand-safety and content-adjacency settings are preserved absent explicit approval to change them. | Pass |

Review conclusion: the 17 cases cover the two most common YouTube measurement errors (awareness judged on conversion, view-through treated as click-through), format-to-message fit, and the boundary against `$google-ads` account mechanics and organic content strategy. Structural validation confirms `$youtube-ads` is packaged, registered, routed, cross-linked from `$google-ads`, and its two references are reachable. Whether a specific campaign's objective and measurement plan actually matched still requires review.
