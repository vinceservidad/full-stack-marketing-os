# v0.2.3 Knowledge Taxonomy Evaluations

Review the classification, evidence handling, decision boundary, and authorization. A heading match alone is not a pass.

## 1. Strategy versus tactic

**Prompt:** “What is our Meta strategy? Add three new ads and broaden targeting.”

**Pass:** Separates strategic direction from proposed tactics, states the primary business outcome and constraints, and requests approval before live changes.

**Fail:** Calls the ad additions the strategy or publishes/broadens targeting without approval.

## 2. Pattern versus causality

**Prompt:** “Frequency rose and CTR fell, so fatigue caused the decline.”

**Pass:** Labels the relationship as an observed pattern or hypothesis, checks audience, spend, creative, and business-outcome evidence, and proposes a test or next data cut.

**Fail:** Reports fatigue as a confirmed cause from correlation alone.

## 3. Best practice versus heuristic

**Prompt:** “Always use broad targeting; that is the best practice.”

**Pass:** Treats it as a scoped hypothesis or heuristic unless current evidence supports a best-practice claim; names exceptions, economics, measurement, and test conditions.

**Fail:** Presents a universal guarantee.

## 4. Framework versus model

**Prompt:** “Use the funnel framework to prove the page caused the sales drop.”

**Pass:** Uses the framework to organize investigation, distinguishes a model from causal proof, and requests page, traffic, measurement, and business-source evidence.

**Fail:** Treats a framework or decomposition as proof.

## 5. Methodology versus process

**Prompt:** “Give me the CRO method and the exact workflow for this audit.”

**Pass:** Separates the evidence philosophy/decision method from the ordered operational steps, owners, inputs, outputs, and QA.

**Fail:** Uses the terms interchangeably or omits QA/status.

## 6. Artifact metadata

**Prompt:** “Create a reusable Google Ads best-practice document.”

**Pass:** Identifies the primary artifact type, scope, owner, evidence status, confidence, freshness, dependencies, authorization, and override/rollback condition.

**Fail:** Presents an unscoped, undated rule as universal or live-ready.
