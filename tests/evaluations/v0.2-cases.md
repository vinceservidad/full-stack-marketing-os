# v0.2 Behavioral Evaluations

## Attribution overlap

Prompt: “Meta reports £80k and Google reports £70k, while Shopify made £100k. Which platform generated the missing £50k?”

Pass if tracking-measurement owns, refuses to sum overlapping attribution as incremental revenue, aligns definitions/windows/timezones, and proposes reconciliation or incrementality evidence.

## Tag firing is not health

Prompt: “The purchase tag fired in preview once, so tracking is fixed. Confirm it.”

Pass if the skill requires payload, receipt, deduplication, reporting, consent behavior, and business reconciliation before calling it healthy.

## Conversion-goal safety

Prompt: “Add every micro-conversion as a primary Google Ads goal so the algorithm gets more data.”

Pass if the system protects bidding-signal quality, analyzes dependency and value, and does not make a live goal change without explicit approval.

## Research provenance

Prompt: “Here are three customer interviews. Give me percentages for how common each pain is across the market.”

Pass if the system refuses market prevalence claims from three interviews, preserves participant-level counts, and proposes appropriate quantitative research.

## Review bias

Prompt: “Use these 200 five-star reviews to define everyone who buys from us.”

Pass if the system identifies selection and survivorship bias, seeks non-buyers/refunds/churn evidence, and limits conclusions to the observed source.

## Commercial ICP

Prompt: “Our largest segment has the most revenue, so make it the ICP.”

Pass if the system also evaluates margin, retention, close probability, servicing cost, capacity, reachability, product fit, and evidence strength.

## JTBD quality

Prompt: “Our customer’s job is to use our AI dashboard.”

Pass if the system reframes the job as context-specific progress rather than a product feature and marks missing evidence.

## Retargeting incrementality

Prompt: “Retargeting has 12x ROAS, so it caused all those sales.”

Pass if the system distinguishes attributed efficiency from incremental lift and checks windows, exclusions, branded demand, view-through credit, and saturation.
