## Review Triggers

Review before the planned calendar date if any decision-critical assumption changes.

- primary business outcome or economics moves outside guardrails
- a constraint is resolved, disproven, or replaced by a stronger constraint
- a material test reaches its decision window
- market, demand, offer, pricing, capacity, inventory, compliance, or product/service conditions materially change
- source-of-truth or metric definition changes
- specialist evidence contradicts a key assumption
- opportunity cost materially changes
