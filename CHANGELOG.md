# Changelog

Notable changes follow semantic versioning.

## [0.2.2] - 2026-08-22

### Added

- Platform-currency contract defining evidence states, freshness gates, source requirements, rollout verification, and a current-platform response format.
- Dated Google Ads and Meta Ads registries with first-party sources, stable-concept/current-label mappings, and high-change watchlists.
- Regression evaluations and deterministic validation for stale registries, undocumented algorithm claims, account-specific availability, and vendor-claim separation.

### Changed

- Google Ads, Meta Ads, and the marketing router now require currency checks for current AI, automation, control, reporting, and interface claims.
- Platform behavior is labeled as officially documented, account-visible, experimentally observed, inferred, or unknown.

## [0.2.1] - 2026-08-22

### Added

- Canonical marketing glossary covering commercial outcomes, measurement, causality, audience and journey concepts, paid media, creative, lead lifecycle, evidence, and experimentation.
- Terminology regression evaluations for finance, Google Ads, Meta Ads, CRO, research, lead generation, attribution, and creative entities.

### Changed

- Standardized “primary business outcome” versus Google Ads “Primary conversion action.”
- Defined gross sales, net revenue, gross profit, contribution profit before/after media, contribution margin, operating profit, ROAS, CAC, and realized revenue.
- Mapped Meta strategic categories to current performance-goal, conversion-location, dataset/event, Advantage+ audience, and Custom Audience language.
- Standardized new-customer acquisition/prospecting and retargeting/remarketing terminology.
- Defined full-funnel, message scent, funnel stage, awareness level, audience temperature, lifecycle stage, and Voice of Customer.
- Corrected contribution formulas to prevent discount/refund double-counting.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-08-20

### Added

- Tracking and measurement skill with event-integrity and attribution-reconciliation references.
- Customer research skill with source-grading and interview-synthesis references.
- ICP/JTBD skill for commercially grounded segment selection.
- Google Search, Shopping, and Performance Max references.
- Meta prospecting and retargeting references.
- Behavioral evaluations for attribution, event QA, research provenance, and segment economics.

### Changed

- Marketing router now covers measurement, research, and ICP/JTBD requests.
- Google Ads and Meta Ads skills progressively route to mode-specific references.
- README and roadmap now reflect v0.2 completion.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-20

### Added

- Marketing router with intent, risk, and composition rules.
- Google Ads, Meta Ads, creative strategy, CRO, and performance diagnostics skills.
- Shared measurement and experimentation frameworks.
- Ecommerce and lead-generation playbooks.
- Audit, experiment, and reporting templates.
- Behavioral evaluation cases and roadmap.
