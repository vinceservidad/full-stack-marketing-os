# Full-Stack Marketing OS v1.0.0

## Overview

The first public release establishes the core operating system structure for AI-assisted marketing workflows.

## Included

- Marketing architecture and decision system
- Specialist skills for paid acquisition, Google Ads, Meta Ads, CRO, and creative strategy
- Reusable templates for audits, briefs, reporting, and experiments
- Workflow systems for launch, optimization, testing, and analysis
- Evaluation systems for quality and decision consistency
- Practical marketing examples

## Design Principles

- Evidence before assumptions
- Strategy before tactics
- Business goals before platform settings
- Testing before scaling
- Human judgment alongside automation

## Next Versions

Future releases will expand specialist skills, industry playbooks, integrations, and evaluation scenarios.
