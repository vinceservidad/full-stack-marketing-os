## Quality Checks

Before adding new systems:

- Does it solve a real marketing decision problem?
- Is the process reusable?
- Is the framework clear?
- Can an AI agent follow the workflow?
- Can the output be evaluated?

## Operating Principle

The goal is not to automate marketing judgment. The goal is to provide structured systems that improve consistency, analysis, and execution.
