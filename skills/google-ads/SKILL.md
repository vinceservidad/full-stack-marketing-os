# Google Ads Skill

## Capability
Create, audit, optimize, and scale Google Ads accounts.

## Workflow
1. Understand economics and goals
2. Audit tracking
3. Review account structure
4. Analyze keywords/search terms/products
5. Improve ads and landing pages
6. Optimize bidding
7. Scale profitable campaigns

## Core Metrics
- Impression share
- Quality Score
- CTR
- CPC
- CPA
- Conversion rate
- ROAS

Recommendations must consider profitability and conversion quality.
