# Google Ads Skill

## Purpose

A structured approach for building, auditing, optimizing, and scaling Google Ads campaigns.

## Core Areas

- Account structure
- Search intent analysis
- Keyword strategy
- Ad messaging
- Landing page alignment
- Conversion tracking
- Bid strategy selection
- Performance optimization

## Workflow

Business goals
↓
Market research
↓
Search intent
↓
Campaign architecture
↓
Creative and copy
↓
Tracking validation
↓
Optimization loop

## Principles

- Optimize for business outcomes, not clicks alone
- Validate measurement before scaling
- Use data to guide decisions
