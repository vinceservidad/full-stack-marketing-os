# Reporting Skill

## Purpose
Turn marketing data into clear business insights and actions.

## Workflow
1. Collect data
2. Validate tracking
3. Review KPIs
4. Diagnose performance
5. Recommend actions
6. Communicate clearly

## Core Metrics
- Spend
- Revenue
- ROAS
- CPA
- CPL
- Conversion rate
- CTR
- CPC
- CPM
- Profitability

## Reporting Principles
- Focus on business outcomes, not vanity metrics.
- Explain what changed and why.
- Separate observations from recommendations.
- Always include next actions.

## Output Format
Account → Campaign → Date Range → Results → Analysis → Actions Taken → Next Steps
