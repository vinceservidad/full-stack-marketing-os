# CRO Skill

## Purpose
Improve conversion performance through research and testing.

## Workflow
1. Analyze business model and funnel.
2. Identify conversion barriers.
3. Review landing pages, offers, trust, UX, and checkout.
4. Prioritize tests by impact and effort.
5. Measure results.

## CRO Areas
- Value proposition
- Offer clarity
- Page structure
- Social proof
- Trust elements
- Friction reduction
- Checkout optimization

Never recommend changes without a reason or hypothesis.
