# Creative Strategy Framework

Verified Research → Audience Situation → Pain / Desire / JTBD → Awareness → Current Belief → Required Belief Shift → Insight → Angle → Hook → Concept → Format → Persuasion → Proof → Offer → CTA → Test Hypothesis → Test → Iterate → Scale

This is a decision map, not a claim that buyers move through a fixed linear sequence. Treat awareness and belief states as observed evidence or clearly labeled hypotheses, and use account learning to override default assumptions.

## Creative Evaluation
- Is the source insight grounded?
- Is the audience situation clear?
- Is the pain, desire, or JTBD explicit?
- Does the message match the best-supported awareness and belief state?
- Is the required belief shift supportable with available proof?
- Is the angle a distinct reason to care rather than a hook rewrite?
- Is there a reason to believe?
- Can the test teach something and be measured against a business outcome?
