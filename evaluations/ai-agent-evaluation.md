# AI Agent Evaluation

## Purpose

Evaluate whether an AI marketing workflow produces useful, reliable decisions.

## Evaluation Criteria

### Context Understanding
- Understands business goal
- Understands customer and market
- Identifies constraints

### Strategic Thinking
- Uses appropriate framework
- Connects actions to outcomes
- Avoids isolated metric optimization

### Execution Quality
- Provides clear next steps
- Defines measurement
- Includes testing approach

### Output Quality
- Accurate
- Practical
- Easy to execute
- Clear assumptions
