# Campaign Quality Checklist

A practical evaluation layer for marketing execution.

## Strategy
- Is the business goal clear?
- Is the target customer defined?
- Is the offer aligned with customer intent?

## Tracking
- Are conversions configured correctly?
- Is data reliable enough for decisions?

## Execution
- Does campaign structure match intent?
- Are ads and landing pages aligned?

## Optimization
- Are decisions based on meaningful data?
- Are tests documented and measurable?
