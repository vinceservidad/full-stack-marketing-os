# Marketing OS Maintenance

## Purpose

Keep the system accurate, useful, and consistent over time.

## Maintenance Areas

### Knowledge

Review frameworks, skills, and playbooks as marketing platforms and practices change.

### Quality

Maintain evaluation criteria and remove outdated assumptions.

### Examples

Add practical examples from real marketing scenarios.

### Documentation

Keep workflows, templates, and architecture aligned.

## Update Rules

- Validate before adding.
- Preserve structure.
- Document major changes.
- Keep business outcomes as the priority.
