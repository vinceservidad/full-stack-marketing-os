# Public Positioning Guide

## Project Positioning

Full-Stack Marketing OS is an open marketing intelligence system combining AI agents, skills, frameworks, playbooks, templates, and evaluation methods for modern growth teams.

## How to Describe It

Short version:

> Building an AI-native marketing operating system with specialized agents for paid media, creative strategy, CRO, SEO, analytics, and reporting.

Long version:

> Full-Stack Marketing OS is a framework-driven marketing system designed for AI agents and marketing teams. It connects strategic thinking, execution workflows, reusable templates, and quality checks into one operating layer.

## Social Media Positioning

Good examples:

- Building an open-source AI marketing operating system.
- Created a framework library for Meta Ads, Google Ads, CRO, SEO, creative strategy, and analytics.
- Combining marketing expertise with AI agent workflows.

Avoid:

- Claiming AI replaces marketers.
- Claiming guaranteed results.
- Sharing confidential client information.

## Public vs Private

Public:
- Skills
- General frameworks
- Templates
- Agent architecture
- Educational examples

Private:
- Client data
- Account screenshots
- Internal experiments
- Confidential strategies
- Proprietary business information
