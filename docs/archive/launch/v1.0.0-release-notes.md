# Full-Stack Marketing OS v1.0.0 Release Notes

## Overview

Full-Stack Marketing OS is an evidence-led marketing system designed for AI agents and marketers. It combines skills, frameworks, playbooks, templates, and evaluations into one operating structure.

## Included

- Marketing router and specialist agents
- Paid media skills for Google Ads and Meta Ads
- Creative strategy and copywriting systems
- CRO and SEO workflows
- Analytics and reporting methods
- Reusable templates and examples
- Evaluation rules for quality control

## Design Principles

- Strategy before tactics
- Evidence before assumptions
- Business goals before platform settings
- Human review before external actions
- Frameworks over random prompts

## Future Roadmap

- More specialist skills
- More industry playbooks
- Expanded evaluation datasets
- More AI agent integrations
