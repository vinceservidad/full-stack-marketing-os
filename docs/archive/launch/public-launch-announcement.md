# Public Launch Announcement

## Full-Stack Marketing OS

An AI-native marketing system built to make marketing strategy more structured, repeatable, and evidence-led.

Instead of relying on isolated prompts, this project organizes marketing knowledge into:

- Agents
- Skills
- Frameworks
- Playbooks
- Templates
- Evaluations

The goal is to help AI systems support real marketing workflows across:

- Paid media
- Creative strategy
- CRO
- SEO
- Analytics
- Reporting

## Why I Built This

Marketing decisions require more than generating ideas. They require context, research, measurement, testing, and business judgment.

This project creates a structured layer between AI capabilities and practical marketing execution.

## Version 1.0 Goals

- Make marketing workflows reusable
- Improve consistency of AI-assisted work
- Keep decisions connected to evidence and measurement
- Create a foundation for specialized marketing agents

## Future Direction

The system will continue evolving with new skills, frameworks, examples, and evaluation methods.
