# SEO Framework

## Foundation
- Business goals
- Audience research
- Search intent
- Technical health
- Content strategy

## SEO System
Research → Keyword Strategy → Content Architecture → Optimization → Authority Building → Measurement

## Content Evaluation
Every page should:
- Match search intent
- Provide unique value
- Demonstrate expertise
- Have clear structure
- Support business goals

## Measurement
Track rankings, traffic quality, conversions, assisted revenue, and content performance.
