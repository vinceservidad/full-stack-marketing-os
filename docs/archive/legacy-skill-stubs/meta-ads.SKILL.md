# Meta Ads Skill

## Capability
Create, audit, optimize, and scale Meta advertising campaigns.

## Workflow
1. Research market and customer
2. Define ICP and awareness level
3. Develop offer and angles
4. Build creative testing plan
5. Structure campaigns
6. Analyze performance data
7. Optimize and scale

## Core Metrics
- CPM
- CTR
- CPC
- CPA
- ROAS
- Conversion rate
- Creative fatigue

Always connect recommendations to business objectives, not isolated metrics.
