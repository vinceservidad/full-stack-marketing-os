# Public vs Private Boundary

## Public Repository

The public repository should showcase:

- Marketing frameworks
- Generalized skills
- Methodologies
- Templates
- Educational examples
- AI workflow structure

## Keep Private

Keep private:

- Client confidential information
- Internal campaign data
- Account access details
- Proprietary experiments
- Private optimization notes
- Unreleased business strategies

## Principle

The goal is to share the system and thinking while protecting confidential information and competitive advantages.
