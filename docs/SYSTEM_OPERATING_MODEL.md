# Marketing OS Operating Model

## Purpose

This document explains how the Full-Stack Marketing OS turns a marketing problem into an actionable workflow.

## Operating Flow

```text
Business Goal
      ↓
Market Understanding
      ↓
Customer Intent
      ↓
Strategy Selection
      ↓
Skill Selection
      ↓
Framework Application
      ↓
Execution Plan
      ↓
Measurement
      ↓
Optimization
      ↓
Learning
```

## Core Principles

- Start with business outcomes, not platform settings.
- Use evidence before assumptions.
- Choose frameworks based on the problem.
- Test ideas with measurable outcomes.
- Document learnings for future decisions.

## System Layers

### Knowledge
Skills, frameworks, and marketing concepts.

### Execution
Templates, workflows, and examples.

### Quality
Evaluations and decision checks.

### Improvement
Experiments, learnings, and system updates.
